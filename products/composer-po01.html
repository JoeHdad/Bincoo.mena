Highlights

Designed to impress. Built to perform.

01

Precise temperature, every moment.

A PID intelligent algorithm adjusts in milliseconds, holding water temperature constant from 40 to 95 °C. Stable output makes every extraction just right.

  • Instant heating — no waiting
  • Dynamic brewing system for even extraction
02

Three pour techniques, endless flavour.

One-pour, three-stage and the four-to-six method — each controls water rhythm and extraction time differently, adapting to every bean for a rich, full-bodied cup.

  • Six preset modes on the touch panel

Connect an external pressureless water line and say goodbye to frequent refills — ideal for studios, offices and busy service. A magnetic drip tray and conical filter cup keep cleaning simple.
